# Wanderwater

An underwater museum built like the classic water ring toss toy. Pump water to float rings onto famous landmarks from ten cities, collect a stamp in your passport for each one, and tap anything in the sea to explore.

## Cities

Paris, Dubai, Delhi, New York, Tokyo, London, Florence, Amsterdam, Oslo and The Hague. Each gallery has three landmarks with their own animation and sound, its own generated music, a famous artwork on the easel, and twelve city facts from the turtle.

## Play

- Press **A** and **L**, or the yellow pump buttons, to shoot water up from the vents.
- Hold the current arrows (or the arrow keys) to push the rings sideways.
- Loop all three landmarks to earn the city's passport stamp.
- Tap landmarks, the pearl, starfish and the easel to learn about them.

## Run locally

It is a single static page with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```
npx serve .
```
