    Wanderwater

    Wander the world, under water.
    Fifteen cities. One very soggy museum.

    • PUMPPump the water, float the rings
    • Ring all three landmarks
    • Tap anything. Everything has a story.

    Yes, that toy.

    A little box of water, a few pegs, a flurry of rings. You mash the two buttons, the water whooshes up, and you hope a ring lands. Usually it doesn't. When it does, it's glorious.

    Wanderwater is that same toy, now with an underwater museum of fifteen cities inside.

    Illustration of the classic toy.

    How to play

    The water toy you loved, with fifteen cities hiding inside.

    1. PumpPress the two pump buttons, or A and L. Water rockets up from the seabed and takes the rings with it. Hold a current, or the arrow keys, to nudge things sideways.
    2. Ring the landmarksDrop a ring onto the tip of a landmark and it slides down and stays. Ring all three to win the city's stamp. Fewer pumps, more bragging rights.
    3. Keyboard
