    How to play

    A water ring toy, turned into an underwater museum of ten cities.

    1. Pump the waterPress A and L, or the yellow buttons. Water shoots straight up from the two vents on the seabed and fans out as it rises, carrying everything with it. Hold Current ◀ or ▶ (arrow keys) to push things sideways.
    2. Loop the landmarksLet a ring drop onto the tip of a landmark. It slides down and stays. Loop all three landmarks to earn the city's passport stamp, in as few pumps as you can.
    3. Keyboard

    4. ExploreTap a landmark to light it up, hear its sound and read its story. Tap the diver to say hi. Tap anything else in the sea too, and drag to stir the water.
